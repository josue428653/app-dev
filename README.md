# My Favorite Anime: Attack on Titan

## About the Series

**Attack on Titan** is a Japanese
dark fantasy anime series based on the manga written
and illustrated by Hajime Isayama.

The story takes place in a world where humanity lives
inside enormous walls to protect themselves from
giant humanoid creatures called Titans.

### Why I Like This Anime

- Amazing storyline and plot twists
- Beautiful animation and fight scenes
- Emotional and unforgettable moments
- Great music and soundtrack

### My Favorite Characters

1. Eren Yeager
2. Mikasa Ackerman
3. Armin Arlert
4. Levi Ackerman

**Genre:** Action, Dark Fantasy, Drama

**Author:** Hajime Isayama
